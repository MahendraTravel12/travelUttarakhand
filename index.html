About us

At Untracked Trails, we believe the best stories aren't found on crowded summits, but on the paths few have traveled. We specialize in new, lesser-known, and less-crowded trails across Uttarakhand, ensuring your journey into the mountains is one of peace, solitude, and raw natural beauty

Hikes

Munsyari & Kholiya Top

A calm track featuring slow meadows, this 4-5 day experience offers a serene escape. For a truly unique perspective, we offer a helicopter experience to witness the grand scale of the Himalayas.

Milam Glacier & Nanda Devi Base Camp

A 7-day deep dive into the heart of the mountains, providing a close view of India’s second-highest sacred peak.

Kasar Devi & Binsar

Perfect for spiritual seekers and nature lovers looking for quiet forests, these routes are fully customizable to your schedule.

Pindari Glacier Trek

A secluded glacial adventure meticulously designed to ensure you experience the grandeur of the mountains without the rush

Panchachuli Base Camp and Om Parvat

The Panchachuli trek is an easy-to-moderate 4-5 day journey from Delhi specifically noted for providing the best snow peak views you can experience, while both expeditions are supported by end-to-end services including transportation, experienced local guides, and authentic Uttarakhand cuisine
