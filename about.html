Who we are

We are the children of these mountains. Born and raised in the heart of Uttarakhand, the Himalayas are not just a destination for us; they are our home. At Untracked Trails, we believe that the true spirit of trekking is found in solitude and discovery. Our mission is to take you away from the "blockbuster" crowded routes and introduce you to new, lesser-known, and less-crowded trails that offer a raw, untouched connection with nature. We are driven by the philosophy that a trek is more than a physical journey—it is a mindfully designed experience that transforms you

What We do

    We specialize in providing bespoke and fully customizable trekking experiences in Uttarakhand. Whether you are looking for an easy stepping stone like the Panchachuli Base Camp or a challenging 7-day expedition to the Milam Glacier, we design our itineraries based on your specific budget and days of travel (Conversation History)

    Our team provides end-to-end services, handling every detail of your journey so you can focus on the mountains:
  • Bespoke Logistics: We manage all transportation and comfortable accommodations.
  • Authentic Flavors: We serve authentic Uttarakhand cuisine, freshly prepared to nourish you and provide a true taste of the region.
  • Exclusive Access: We operate with strictly limited slots to maintain high standards of safety and intimacy (Conversation History).
  • Sustainability: We practice a "Leave No Trace" philosophy, ensuring we leave the mountains cleaner than we found them
